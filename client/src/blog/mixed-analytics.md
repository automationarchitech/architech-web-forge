# Mixed Analytics: Data-Driven Content Strategy for SaaS Growth

## Client/Company
Mixed Analytics

## Problem
Growing SaaS product needed strategic support with enterprise customer acquisition and content marketing to drive user growth.

## Solution
Analyzed GA4 data to identify high-value data sources used by the application, then created 14 targeted videos and website content to drive organic traffic to their Google Sheets Add-On ([YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCuNKF6uPXIwBHzW9rdMWzkQ)).

## Impact
- Grew MAU from **200k to 1M+** (400% increase) in 12 months
- Built YouTube channel from **0 to 2,000+ subscribers** with 12 videos
- Drove organic traffic through data-driven content strategy
